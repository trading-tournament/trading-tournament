New to trading?

Trading means making a decision about whether you think a market price may rise or fall. Trading League lets you practice with demo money.

1. Choose a market Pick something like Apple, Tesla or Bitcoin.
2. Look at the chart The chart shows how the price has moved over time.
3. Choose BUY or SELL BUY means you expect the price to rise. SELL means you expect the price to fall.
